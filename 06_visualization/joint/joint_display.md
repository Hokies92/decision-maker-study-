Generated by `scripts/p6_build_joint_display.py` from `inventory/concept_links.csv`, the coding frame, the codes, the corpus, T01 and T02. Do not edit by hand. **Every link, judgement and pillar statement is INFERRED until Scott reviews it (P6.S12).**

Methods: joint display (Fetters, Curry & Creswell 2013), following a thread (Moran-Ellis et al. 2006), convergence coding (Farmer et al. 2006), pillar integration (Johnson, Grove & Clarke 2019). Method register QL04, QL06–QL08.

**Unit and limits.** Survey results are shares of all respondents (n = 319), group tables only. Written results are counts of answers. The two never share an axis and are never subtracted or divided (R01, R08). Sources join by concept only, never by respondent (R04): no respondent has a role level or stakeholder type. A concept is a code in the behaviour or lexicon layer of the coding frame.

**58 concepts:** agree 7, expand 12, silent: survey 20, silent: written 18, silent: both 1.

## How each concept is judged

- **Survey reading.** Valued items (Q1, Q7, Q11, Q13, Q19: what decision-makers say they want) read *most* at 55% or more, else *a minority*. Received items (every other question: what they say they get) read *common* at 55% or more, else *uncommon*. The line is Scott's (P6.S12). A whole question has no single result and is not read.
- **Written reading.** Behaviour codes take their P5 class from best and worst accounts (satisfier, dissatisfier, critical, neutral, too few). Lexicon codes carry no direction. Fewer than 10 answers = silent.
- **Per item.** Valued and most → agree; valued and a minority → expand. Received, where the code and the item share a pole: common + satisfier → agree; common + dissatisfier → disagree; uncommon + dissatisfier or critical → agree; uncommon + satisfier → expand; otherwise expand. A received item linked to a lexicon code, or only through a lexicon entry, → expand (no direction to compare); a valued item is read the same way for every code. An item that measures the other pole (the pitch, for *puts my needs first*) has its reading flipped.
- **Per concept.** Disagree if any item disagrees; agree if agreeing items are at least as many as expanding ones; else expand. Silent when one side is absent. An option set of one question (e.g. Q14 options 3 + 4 + 5) counts once; a share within 5 points of the 55% line is flagged in the judgement, because its reading could tip either way.
