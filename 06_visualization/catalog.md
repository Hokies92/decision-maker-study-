### V4 — What wins, what loses

Sources: `05_open_ended/views/coded/kano.csv`, `05_open_ended/codes.csv`, `05_open_ended/coding_frame.md`, `05_open_ended/corpus.csv`

- Effective = coded + in a best account (Q22, Q24); ineffective = coded − in a worst account (Q23, Q25).
- Class (Johnston 1995 rule, adapted): of ≥ 10 experiential mentions, ≥ 2/3 ineffective = dissatisfier; ≥ 2/3 effective = satisfier; ≥ 1/4 each = critical; else neutral.
- Codes are one coder's, checked blind on 200 answers (behaviour alpha 0.87).

Provenance: Codes INFERRED. Answers VERBATIM.
